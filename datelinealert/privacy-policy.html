Dateline Alert Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Dateline Alert ("the app") is designed to help users manage important datelines and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Dateline Alert.

1. Information Stored by the App

Dateline Alert allows you to create and manage information such as incident names, descriptions, dates, times, and reminder settings.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's reminder, notification, alarm, history, and related functionality.

Dateline Alert does not require you to create an account.

2. Notifications and Alarms

Dateline Alert may schedule notifications and alarms according to the reminders you create. These functions may operate while the app is running in the background.

The app uses Android system notification and alarm functionality to provide these reminders.

3. Advertising

Dateline Alert uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information from your device for purposes such as advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on applicable settings and consent requirements.

Information collected by Google through its advertising services is handled according to Google's own privacy policies.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Data Sharing

Dateline Alert does not sell your personal information.

The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect information directly from the app or device as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Security

Information stored locally by Dateline Alert is subject to the security provided by your Android device and operating system.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete information created within Dateline Alert using the deletion features provided by the app. You may also uninstall the app to remove its locally stored application data, subject to Android device behavior and backup settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Dateline Alert is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Dateline Alert, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
