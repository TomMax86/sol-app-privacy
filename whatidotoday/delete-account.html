Account Deletion

What I Do Today allows users to request permanent deletion of their application accounts and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You do not need to reinstall or sign in to the application to submit an external deletion request.

Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Account Deletion Request form using the button below.
  2. Enter the Google account email address used to sign in to What I Do Today.
  3. Provide a contact email if it is different from your Google account email.
  4. Confirm your deletion request and submit the form.
Request Account Deletion

What Happens After Submission?

Your request will be reviewed and your account ownership may be verified before deletion is processed.

We will use the submitted information only for handling and responding to your deletion request.

Any information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons will be handled according to applicable requirements.

Important: Never provide your Google password or verification codes in an account deletion request.
