Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Sol Lim ("we", "us" or "our") operates the What I Do Today mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use our application.

By using What I Do Today, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

What I Do Today requires users to sign in using Google Sign-In before accessing the application.

Information obtained through Google Sign-In includes:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile picture

Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. These account details are used to authenticate users and maintain their application accounts.

Users may also enter the following information into the app:

  • Activity names
  • Activity categories
  • Activity dates
  • Start times, end times and durations
  • Category and group information
  • Application preferences and settings

This activity information is stored locally on the user's device. The current version does not upload activity records to Firebase Firestore or provide cloud synchronisation.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users through Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain and manage user accounts.
  • Record and organise daily activities.
  • Calculate time allocations and activity summaries.
  • Display daily activity statistics and charts.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Respond to account deletion and privacy requests.

3. Local Storage and Device Backup

Activity records, categories, groups and application preferences are stored in the application's local storage on your Android device.

The application supports offline activity tracking after successful sign-in.

Android application backup is enabled. Depending on your Android device and backup settings, application data may be included in Android backup or device-transfer processes. These processes are managed by Android and its associated backup services.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

What I Do Today uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and interstitial advertisements on selected screens.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain information, including:

  • IP address
  • Advertising and device identifiers
  • Interactions with advertisements
  • Diagnostic information
  • Other device or account-related information used for advertising purposes

This information may be used for advertising delivery, advertising measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and settings.

The current application version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK's default advertising request configuration.

We do not use Firebase Analytics or a separate crash-reporting service in the current version.

Relevant third-party privacy information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google AdMob and advertising information

5. Information We Do Not Collect

The current version of What I Do Today does not use device location tracking, health monitoring, financial services, notification reminders or separate analytics and crash-reporting services.

The app does not provide an activity export or external activity-sharing feature.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' activity records or account profile information.

Information may be processed by the following service providers to support application functionality:

  • Google and Firebase: For Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • Google Mobile Ads: For displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Google Forms: For receiving external account deletion requests.

We do not currently use Firebase Firestore to upload or synchronise users' activity records.

7. Data Retention

Account information is retained while the user's application account remains active.

Activity records and preferences are stored locally until they are deleted by the user, removed through account deletion, or otherwise removed from the device.

Android backup copies may be subject to the retention and deletion practices of the relevant backup service.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request permanent deletion of their What I Do Today account.

Account deletion can be initiated from within the app or through our external account deletion webpage.

When account deletion is completed, the Firebase Authentication account and associated application data will be deleted.

To submit an external deletion request, visit:

What I Do Today - Account Deletion Request

We may request additional information to verify ownership of the account before processing a request.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application.

Account authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. Locally stored application information is maintained within the application's Android storage.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

What I Do Today is intended for general users and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly design the application to collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, its services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this webpage with a revised update date.
